Recording inspector

Signal measurements of a 16-bit PCM WAV file, computed inside this page. The file is not uploaded: the page's security policy blocks every network connection.

For recordings of Pirahã, the page also shows phoneme and word candidates from an experimental model. They are a machine's reading of the sound, not a transcription.

A word candidate is a stretch whose phoneme scores resemble an entry of the word list; the fit is 0 when the model reads exactly that entry and is sure of it. The list knows only its own entries, so other words can show up as the entry they most resemble. Glosses are quoted from the list.

Results as files

The tables are time-coded measurements of the recording. Keep them with the same care as the recording itself, outside the repository.

The measurements above are of the signal: they do not identify speech, speakers or words. No candidate on this page, of a phoneme or of a word, is a validated annotation.
