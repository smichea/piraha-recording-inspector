1. What this site is

A noncommercial research project offering a web page that measures audio recordings in your browser, an experimental phoneme model for the Pirahã language, and notes about how they were made. Using the site means you accept these terms. If you do not, please do not use it.

2. Experimental results, no warranty

The phoneme and word candidates are a machine's reading of sound. They are not transcriptions, translations or linguistic findings, and no speaker of the language has checked them. Do not present them as such, and do not rely on them for any decision that matters.

The site and its software are provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, and may change or disappear without notice. To the extent the law allows, the project is not liable for any damage arising from their use.

3. Your recordings

The recording inspector reads the file you choose inside your browser. The page does not upload it, and its security policy forbids it any network connection. Two things are outside the page's control: your browser itself, and any browser extension allowed to read pages. For a sensitive recording, use a browser profile without extensions.

You are responsible for having the right to open and analyse the recordings you choose, and for respecting the people recorded in them. Result files you save are derived from your recording: treat them with the same care.

What you mark and type in the inspector (verdicts on candidates, transcriptions, translations, notes) stays in the page. It never reaches this site, and the page keeps nothing in your browser once it is closed: your annotations exist only in the files you save, which are yours.

4. Privacy

This site sets no cookies, has no accounts, and runs no analytics or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages: like any web host, GitHub receives technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads the pages, under GitHub's privacy statement. The project does not receive or keep that data.
