Recording inspector
===================

A page that measures a 16-bit PCM WAV file inside the browser. The file a
visitor chooses is not uploaded: the page's security policy forbids it every
network connection, and the analysis module has no access to the network.

Phoneme model and word list
---------------------------

phoneme_model.js and phoneme_words.js are derived from the Pirahã (MYP)
recordings and word lists of the UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive, recorded in 1995:

    2007. The UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive. Los Angeles, CA: UCLA Department of
    Linguistics. http://archive.phonetics.ucla.edu/

The archive's material is under a Creative Commons licence (Attribution-
NonCommercial 2.0 Generic, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/2.0/),
and its site asks that derivatives be shared under the same terms. These two
files are offered under those terms: noncommercial use, with attribution to
the UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive. The glosses in the word list are quoted from
the archive's word lists.

The model gives candidates, not transcriptions. Its figures and limits are in
the page, under "About the model".
